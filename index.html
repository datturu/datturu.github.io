SARS-CoV-2 Sequence Clustering

Groups SARS-CoV-2 receptor-binding-domain DNA sequences into clusters to find related strains and flag outliers.

Python Ray Tracer

A ray tracer built from scratch with NumPy. It casts a ray through every pixel, finds the closest sphere or plane it hits, and shades it from the surface normal and light direction.

Renders a scene of two spheres on a plane to a PNG, with a step-by-step Jupyter notebook version.

Indus Script Digitization

Deep-learning pipeline that reads the undeciphered Indus Valley script from seal images. YOLOv3 finds each sign, MobileNet identifies it, and a CNN classifies the seal's motif.

Everything is archived in a searchable MySQL database. Published in the Journal of Computer Applications in Archaeology.
